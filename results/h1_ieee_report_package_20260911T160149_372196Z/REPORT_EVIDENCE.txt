V4FinBench final report evidence summary

Task definition:
- Dataset file: company_years_h2.parquet.
- Paper horizon: h=1, one-year-ahead financial distress.
- Rows: 996,500.
- Features: 131.
- Positive records: 3,054.
- Validation protocol: five company-grouped, country-preserving folds.

Primary grouped cross-validation findings:
- LightGBM achieved the best mean F1 (0.182673) and PR-AUC (0.103034).
- LightGBM improved mean F1 by 151.9% and mean PR-AUC by 262.5% relative to SGD Logistic Regression.
- XGBoost had the highest mean ROC-AUC (0.942266), exceeding LightGBM by only 0.001012.
- Logistic Regression produced the highest mean recall but much lower precision, F1, and PR-AUC.

Model policy:
- Primary benchmark model: LightGBM with all 131 official features.
- Timing sensitivity model: LightGBM without operational_status.
- Do not describe the model as deployment-ready.

Temporal evaluation:
- All-feature 2019 test F1: 0.149927.
- All-feature 2019 test ROC-AUC: 0.928130.
- All-feature 2019 test PR-AUC: 0.068697.
- Without-status 2019 test F1: 0.141443; PR-AUC: 0.063249.

Feature-timing audit:
- operational_status was constant for 99.8891% of company groups.
- 99.9502% of rows equaled the company's latest status value.
- The no-status sensitivity model must therefore remain in the report.
- Insolvency_flag and Loss_flag changed across company-year records and did not directly reproduce the target.

Top SHAP features:
- 1. Equity/total_liabilities
- 2. operational_status
- 3. year
- 4. Total_liabilities/total_assets
- 5. Working_capital
- 6. Equity-share_capital/fixed_assets
- 7. Log_revenue/gdp
- 8. Net_profit+depreciation/total_liabilities
- 9. Working_capital/total_liabilities
- 10. Log_operating_profit/gdp

Important interpretation constraints:
- Thresholds were selected on validation data and applied unchanged to test data.
- The temporal test set was not used for threshold retuning.
- 2020 contains zero positive labels and is not used as a temporal test year.
- Country-level results show that one global threshold transfers unevenly across countries; this is reported as a limitation rather than corrected using test labels.
